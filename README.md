AstroMind

Autonomous Astronaut Health & Mission Support System

AstroMind is a smart health and mission support platform designed for astronauts during deep space missions such as the Moon Gateway, Mars missions, and long-duration space exploration missions.

The main goal of AstroMind is to help astronauts stay healthy, safe, and mentally strong while living and working in space. The system continuously collects data from wearable sensors, spacecraft systems, and medical devices to monitor the physical and mental condition of each crew member.

AstroMind can track important health information such as heart rate, blood oxygen level, body temperature, respiration rate, stress level, fatigue, sleep quality, and exercise performance. It also monitors eye health, radiation exposure, cabin air quality, water quality, and other environmental conditions inside the spacecraft.

The platform uses Artificial Intelligence (AI) to analyze real-time data and detect early signs of health problems, stress, fatigue, social isolation, or team conflicts. AstroMind can predict possible risks up to 72 hours in advance and provide recommendations to help astronauts avoid serious issues before they happen.

The system includes an AI Companion that can communicate with astronauts through voice interaction. It provides daily health advice, psychological support, emergency guidance, mission recommendations, and wellness activities such as breathing exercises and relaxation sessions.

AstroMind also supports team management by monitoring communication patterns, crew relationships, and teamwork performance. This helps mission control and astronauts maintain a positive and productive working environment.

Because deep space missions may not always have a stable connection with Earth, AstroMind is designed to work offline. The AI can continue monitoring, analyzing, and supporting astronauts even when communication delays occur.

Overall, AstroMind is an intelligent health and mission support system that combines healthcare, environmental monitoring, predictive analytics, mental wellness support, and AI assistance into one platform. It helps astronauts stay healthy, improve mission performance, and increase safety during long-duration space exploration missions.
